### The store (from Sayan's visit)

- The store Sayan visited is over 28,000 sq ft. **To confirm:** that this is the Raipur store, and the sizes of Bilaspur and Jagdalpur.
- It carries every product category needed to build a house, from mid-range to absolute top luxury.
- What stands out is how products are displayed and segmented. It feels more like an art gallery than a shop.
- The entrance is bright; the store gets darker further in, with spotlit displays.
- Display bays sit along a long walking corridor, each framed, with backlit niches and patterned floor inlays marking zones.
- Outside photography is not allowed in the store.

### Business goals for the website

- Footfall at the stores, especially Raipur.
- Quality leads (visit bookings, calls, WhatsApp enquiries).
- Show the sheer range of products and brands, and the brands they are certified or authorised dealers for.
- Target regions (from the ads brief): all of Chhattisgarh, all of Odisha; Madhya Pradesh (Jabalpur, Bhopal, Indore); Maharashtra (Nagpur, Amravati, Chandrapur, Gondia, Bhandara).

### Important constraint: limited product imagery

Rathi's is a large player, and as soon as a product design is visible online, cheap copies appear in the market. The site must use very few product photos and be clever about what it shows: materials, spaces, people, brand names and numbers rather than whole products.
