Our Brands

The names we stock, grouped by where you will find them in the store. Many sit in more than one category.

Hansgrohe

Taps and showers from Schiltach in the Black Forest, where the company has been engineering water since 1901. Mixers, hand showers and rain heads built around how the water actually feels, not just how it looks.

Kohler

Founded in Wisconsin in 1873 and now one of the largest kitchen and bath names in the world. Sanitaryware, intelligent toilets, basins, mixers and whirlpools, carried here across both the bath and wellness floors.

Fima Carlo Frattini

Italian brassware from the tap-making district around Lake Orta, designing and manufacturing since 1969. Sculptural mixers and shower sets in finishes that run from polished chrome to brushed brass and gunmetal.

Franke

Swiss, and in kitchens since 1911. Sinks, mixers, waste systems and worktop fittings built to commercial-kitchen tolerances, with bath accessories and washroom systems alongside them.

Oyster

A Delhi bath house working the wellness end of the bathroom since 2003: acrylic and freestanding tubs, whirlpools, shower enclosures, steam cabins and the spa systems that go behind them.

Sofpour

The dependable, value-led end of the bath department: everyday fittings, sanitaryware and accessories, specified where a project needs volume without dropping the standard of the fit.

Stonelam

The Indian partner of Italy's Laminam, with ultra-thin porcelain slabs up to 3.2 metres long for floors, walls, façades and worktops — one surface, running almost joint-free across a whole room.

Nexion

Italian porcelain in stone, wood and concrete effects. Through-body tiles and large slabs made for floors that take real traffic and still read as natural material years in.

Haique

Engineered quartz, milled in Udaipur. Haique runs fashion-led veining and colour through full-size slabs for kitchen counters, vanities and islands that have to stay stain-free for twenty years.

Ceramuse

Bespoke ceramic and marble, made to the room rather than to a catalogue. Ceramuse is the craft end of the surfaces floor: matched slabs, cut work and panels made for one wall in one house.

axiSTONE

Sintered stone slabs for kitchens and bathrooms: heat, stain and scratch resistant, in thin large formats that carry a worktop and its splashback in a single piece of material.

axi marble

Marble and marble-look surfaces in large format, book-matched so the veining runs unbroken across a feature wall, a vanity top or the length of a dining table.

Icon

Large-format vitrified tiles and slabs in marble, stone and concrete finishes, for floors and walls that need to run unbroken across a big room with as few lines in them as possible.

Mozart

Full-body vitrified tiles from Morbi, in matt and textured finishes. The colour runs right through the body, so chips and years of traffic never show up as a pale edge.

FlexStone

Real stone, sliced thin and backed so that it bends. Flexible veneer sheets wrap columns, curves and furniture in places a rigid slab simply cannot be made to go.

Thunder Wood

Wood for floors and walls: engineered planks and wood-finish surfaces in oak, walnut and smoked tones, for rooms that want warmth underfoot rather than stone.

Ego

Wood and vinyl floors: engineered boards, laminate planks and luxury vinyl in widths and tones that suit a whole floor plate rather than a single room.

Häfele

German hardware, out of Nagold since 1923. The parts that make furniture work: hinges, drawer runners, lift systems, locks, lighting and the full kitchen and wardrobe fit-out behind the doors.

Vox

A Polish interiors house making wall and ceiling panelling, slat systems, doors and furniture fronts — a finishing system for a whole room rather than a single product line.

RAVS

Designer and decorative veneers, natural and reconstituted, sequence-matched so that a run of doors or a full wardrobe wall reads as one single piece of timber.

Everest Ply

Plywood, blockboards and WPC boards for carcassing, furniture and wet areas — the structure that sits under the finish, in grades from commercial through to marine.

Labacha

Furniture fittings and handles in brass and zinc: cabinet pulls, long profile handles and mortise sets for main doors, in finishes from chrome and antique brass through to rose gold and matt black.

Schüco

The German systems house for aluminium and uPVC. Schüco engineers the profile, the gasket and the hardware as one tested system, which is why it holds against wind, driving rain and forced entry.

Lucasta

Luxury fenestration: slim-framed sliding and openable systems, designed so that the glass does the talking and the frame all but disappears into the reveal.

Eternia

Hindalco's window brand. Aluminium window and door systems made for Indian weather and tested against driving rain, dust and street noise before they go anywhere near a site.
