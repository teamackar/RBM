About Us

Welcome to Rathi’s Build Mart — your one-stop destination for all construction, interior and designing needs.

We believe building your dream space should be a hassle-free journey, and that is why we offer a wide range of products, 100+ brands and unmatched services — all under one roof. Whether you are building a home, an office or a commercial building, we have world-class solutions for all your projects.

We are not in the business to sell products. Our aim is to become your partner in building your dream space.
